Slash Launcher — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · Developer: Sithum Jayasinhe

Slash Launcher is a home-screen launcher app. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device. The short version: Slash Launcher does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your personal data.

Information the app uses on your device only

  • Installed apps list. To work as a launcher, the app reads the names and package names of the apps installed on your phone so it can display and open them. This information never leaves your device.
  • Battery level and next alarm. If you switch on the optional Glance card, the app reads your battery level and the time of your next alarm to display them on your home screen. This happens only on your device, only while the Glance card is enabled.
  • Your settings. Your chosen apps, favourites, theme, emblem word and other preferences are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not transmitted anywhere, and they are removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchases

Slash Launcher offers one optional one-time in-app purchase ("Slash Premium") that unlocks extra styles. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or bank details. The app checks your purchase status with Google Play only so it can unlock premium features and restore a purchase you already made.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking, or usage reporting.
  • No access to your contacts, messages, photos, files, camera, microphone, or location.

Children

Slash Launcher is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
