Privacy Policy — AL Past Papers

Last updated: 2026-11-22

Short version

AL Past Papers does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information about you. There are no accounts and no sign-ins. The app shows ads (banner and native ads) served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. Everything else you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app does

AL Past Papers helps students find G.C.E. Advanced Level past question papers and marking schemes for Sri Lanka, in Sinhala, Tamil and English, from 1999 to 2025. Download a paper once and read it offline; bookmark papers to find them fast.

Data stored on your device

Downloaded papers are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, along with your bookmarks and your language and stream choices. This data:

  • contains no personal information — just your own saved papers and settings;
  • never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else;
  • can be cleared at any time by removing a download in the app or clearing the app's data in Android settings.

Network requests

The app fetches papers from Google Drive, paperzone.lk, and the Department of Examinations' sites (doenets.lk and e-thaksalawa), and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Standard web requests like these expose your device's IP address to those hosts in the same way as visiting any website. We do not log or process this information.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob — banner ads and native ads. There are no full-screen (interstitial) ads. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier) and use it to show you relevant ads. AdMob's data practices are described in Google's partner-sites policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Sources

Papers are published by the Department of Examinations, Sri Lanka — https://www.doenets.lk — and e-thaksalawa. This is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with the Department of Examinations or the Ministry of Education. Check the official site for corrections and notices.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ins — nothing to register.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • No location data collected by us.
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties by us.

Children

AL Past Papers is a general study tool with no user accounts or data collection.
