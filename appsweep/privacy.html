Privacy Policy — AppSweep

Last updated: 16 November 2026

AppSweep is a daily tester for your own apps. It opens each app you pick, gives it a set time on screen, and records whether you marked it passed or flagged.

What AppSweep collects

Nothing. AppSweep collects no personal data, no usage data, and no device data. It has no account, no sign-in, and nothing you do in the app leaves your phone. Your app groups and run results are stored only on your device.

Ads

AppSweep shows ads provided by Google AdMob — a banner on the home screen and one full-screen ad when a run finishes. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show ads. You can read Google's privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

Permissions

AppSweep asks for internet access only, so it can load ads. It asks for no other permissions.

Children

AppSweep is a tool for app developers. It is not aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
