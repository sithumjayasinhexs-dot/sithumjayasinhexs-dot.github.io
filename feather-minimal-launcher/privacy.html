Feather Minimal Launcher — Privacy Policy

Feather Minimal Launcher is an Android home-screen launcher. It collects nothing.

What the app does on your phone: it lists the apps already installed on your device so you can launch them, and it remembers your own choices — your favourite apps, theme, clock size, alignment, and format — in the app's private storage on the device.

What it does not do: it has no internet permission, no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no ads. Nothing about your apps, favourites, or settings leaves your phone. There is nothing to share with anyone, because nothing is collected.

Purchases: the app offers an optional premium subscription, sold and processed entirely by Google Play. Payment details go to Google, never to the app. The app only learns whether the subscription is active, so it can unlock the premium designs.

Changes: if this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here.
