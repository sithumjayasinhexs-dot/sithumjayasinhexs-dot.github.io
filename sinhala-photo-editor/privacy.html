Privacy Policy — Sinhala Photo Editor

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Sinhala Photo Editor is a photo editor that lets you add Sinhala text to your photos. This policy explains what data the app and its advertising partner collect.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, does not collect analytics, and does not send your photos or edits anywhere. Photos you import are edited on your device and saved to your gallery — they are never uploaded to any server.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and use it to show ads that are relevant to you. This data is collected and handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Permissions

The app requests:

  • Internet and network state — to load ads.
  • Camera (optional) — only if you choose to take a photo inside the app. The camera is not required; you can import photos from your gallery instead.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove everything the app holds on your phone, simply uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
